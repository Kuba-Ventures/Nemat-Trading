03 · The Primary Logo
The TT card.

One card, four parts, fixed forever. It leads every application. Reproduce it from master art only; never rebuild, re-space or recolor it.

01 · CARD

Charcoal, fixed corner radius. Everything we sell is a card.

02 · FRAME

One gold inset line. The only gold in the mark.

03 · TT

Two cream Ts of equal width. Never kerned or retyped.

04 · DIAMOND

The red pip. The only red outside warnings.

Clear space
X

Keep at least X clear on every side, where X is the width of one T. No type, photo edge or other mark enters this zone.

Minimum size
✓ 40 px
✕ 24 px

Never smaller than 10 mm tall in print or 40 px on screen. Below that the frame fills in; use the favicon cut.

Size steps
HeroSocialMin

Hero for packaging and the site header, social for avatars, minimum for receipts and fine print.

08 · Iconography · The Devices

Four devices carry the card-shop idea. Each has one job and a few firm rules. Used together and sparingly, they frame the price; used loudly, they cheapen it.

The Frame
Container · Value

A single gold inset line on a card body. It holds whatever matters most on a surface: the price, the product, a principle.

DO
Hold one thing
DON'T
Nest frames or frame body copy
The Pip
Mark · Emphasis

The red diamond from the TT card. One per surface, as a full stop: the end of a card, a list, a signature.

DO
One, sparingly
DON'T
Use as a bullet list or a sale badge
The Fan
Motion · Abundance

Gold outline cards fanned from one corner. Suggests a deck and a drop. Sits behind type, never on top.

DO
Let it pass behind
DON'T
Fill it or fan the TT card itself

02

03
